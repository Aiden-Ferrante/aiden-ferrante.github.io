I'm starting this site for a simple reason: I learn best when I have to explain something, and an
explanation nobody can read doesn't force any honesty.

The plan has two tracks. First, I keep a long-term map of concepts I want to actually understand —
not survey-level familiarity, but the kind of understanding where you can rebuild the thing from
scratch. I'll be making YouTube videos working through that map, and every video gets a companion
post here with the derivations, code, and dead ends that don't fit in a video.

Second, opinions. I spend a lot of time building with and around AI systems, and I have takes on
what's real and what's noise. Those become essays.

If you're a future employer or collaborator reading this: the archive below is the honest signal.
It shows what I chose to learn, how I think when I explain it, and whether I kept showing up.
